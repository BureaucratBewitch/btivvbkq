Mason jar soil layer calculator

After your jar settles, measure each band. This turns heights into sand / silt / clay percentages and compares them to a practical ~40 / 40 / 20 loam target.

Quick reminder

  1. Jar one-third soil, nearly full of water; shake hard; leave overnight.
  2. Sand settles first (bottom), silt next, clay on top once the water clears.
  3. Measure each layer’s height with a ruler (same units). Enter them below.

A jar test is diagnosis, not a lab assay. Organic matter, gravel, and incomplete settling skew the bands. Treat ±5 percentage points as “close enough” for backyard beds — then compost on purpose instead of guessing with miracle bags.
